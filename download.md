# Free Mac download

PitchKiwi is free and open source under MIT. There are no paid plans or pricing page.

- Main button: Download for Mac → `/api/download` → official notarized DMG.
- Requirements: Apple Silicon (M1+), macOS 14+; local English speech model included.
- Install: open the DMG, drag PitchKiwi to Applications, eject, and launch.
- Installation guide: `/download/` with DMG/ZIP/checksum links.
- Source and optional support: star, report issues, or contribute at https://github.com/ayshahawy-cmd/pitchkiwi.
- No email signup, account, subscription, payment, or API key.
