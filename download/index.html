PitchKiwi for Mac

Local voice dictation built for developers, prompts, and coding agents.

Download for Mac macOS 14+ · Apple Silicon

Four quick steps

01

Download and unzip

Download the PitchKiwi ZIP, open it, then drag PitchKiwi.app into your Applications folder.

02

Open the beta safely

In Applications, Control-click PitchKiwi and choose Open, then confirm. This beta is not notarized yet; you do not need to disable Gatekeeper.

03

Grant two permissions

Follow onboarding to allow Microphone access for speech and Accessibility access so PitchKiwi can insert text at your cursor.

04

Start talking

Hold Fn, speak, and release to insert. Double-press Fn for hands-free dictation, then press it again to stop.

Your speech stays local. PitchKiwi runs transcription and cleanup on your Mac. Your microphone audio and dictated text are not sent to PitchKiwi's website.
