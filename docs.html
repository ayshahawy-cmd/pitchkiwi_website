Docs

Everything you need to get PitchKiwi running and comfortable on your Mac.

01Requirements

  • A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer)
  • macOS 14 Sonoma or newer
  • Microphone and Accessibility permission

02Install

  1. Open the download page and get the ZIP and checksum from GitHub Releases. No email, account, or payment is required.
  2. Download and open the ZIP, then drag PitchKiwi.app into Applications.
  3. This beta is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. After trying to open it, use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway and confirm. Older macOS versions may also offer Control-click → Open. Verify the release checksum first.
  4. Grant the two permissions PitchKiwi asks for: Microphone and Accessibility.
  5. Complete the short in-app onboarding, then try dictation in TextEdit.
Why the extra click on first launch? The current beta is not notarized. Follow Apple’s per-app approval instructions after verifying the download.

03First dictation

  1. Click into any text field: TextEdit is a good place to try it first.
  2. Hold the Fn key.
  3. Speak while the small waveform moves with your voice.
  4. Release Fn. Your words appear, cleaned up, at the cursor.

The first transcription after launch is slower while the speech model loads. After that, it's fast.

04Setting your hotkey

Fn is the default. Hold it while speaking and release it to insert text. Double-press Fn for hands-free dictation, then press it again to stop.

Using a non-Apple keyboard? Some third-party keyboards handle Fn in their own firmware, so macOS applications cannot detect it. Use your Mac's built-in keyboard or an Apple keyboard for the Fn workflow.

05Prompt Mode

Select Agent Prompt in Command Deck before dictating with Fn. Choose Short to organize your spoken cues into Goal, Context, Requirements, and Done when. Medium and Detailed append fixed local execution guidance. There is no separate Fn+Shift shortcut or cloud rewrite model. Compare Show original when you want to inspect cleanup.

06Troubleshooting

My old clipboard content gets pasted instead of the transcription.

Use Copy on the latest result and paste manually. Check Accessibility access and keep the destination focused while recognition finishes. There is no adjustable paste-delay preference in this beta. Report the target app/version using synthetic text if the problem persists.

Permissions stopped working after an update.

Unsigned builds can lose their permission grants when the app is rebuilt. Reset and re-grant:

tccutil reset Accessibility com.pitchkiwi.app
tccutil reset Microphone com.pitchkiwi.app

Then relaunch and grant both permissions again in System Settings.
