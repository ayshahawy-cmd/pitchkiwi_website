02Download PitchKiwi

Grab the app and install it on your Mac. Requires a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Download PitchKiwi

03Open it

Drag PitchKiwi into Applications. On first launch, macOS will say it can't verify the developer. This is expected: PitchKiwi isn't signed with a paid Apple developer account yet, so this one extra click is expected and safe. Right-click the app and choose Open, then confirm. You only do this once.

04Enter your license key

Paste the key from your email into the license screen PitchKiwi shows on first launch. That's it, no account, no login.

05Grant two permissions and try it

  • Microphone — so PitchKiwi can hear you talk.
  • Accessibility — so PitchKiwi can insert text into whatever app you're typing into.

Hold Fn in any text field and talk.
