Privacy, Terms & Accessibility

PitchKiwi is a solo-built app. This page says plainly what data exists, where it lives, and what you're agreeing to. It isn't legal advice, and if anything here is unclear, ask before you buy.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

PitchKiwi's speech recognition and text cleanup run entirely on your Mac. Your voice recordings, transcripts, and dictation history are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored anywhere but your own device.

We don't have accounts

There is no PitchKiwi account, no email address on file, no password, and no server of ours holding your data. PitchKiwi is a one-time $16.99 purchase, unlocked with a license key issued by Gumroad.

The one network call the app makes on its own

WhatWhen
License checkOnce when you activate, then roughly every 7 days, to confirm your license key is valid

This request sends only your license key and PitchKiwi's product ID to Gumroad's licensing API — nothing else about you or your Mac. If you're offline, the app stays unlocked for up to 30 days since the last successful check before it asks again.

What never leaves your Mac

  • Audio you record while dictating
  • Transcripts and dictation history
  • Your personal dictionary and snippets
  • Anything typed or spoken into another app through PitchKiwi

Third parties

ServiceWhat it sees
GumroadAt purchase: your email and payment details, to process the sale and issue your license key, under Gumroad's own privacy policy. At activation and every ~7 days after: just your license key and our product ID, via its public licensing API.
huggingface.coA request for a speech model file, only when you choose to download one
Update serverA version check, only if you enable automatic update checks

Gumroad is the merchant of record for your purchase and collects your email and payment info independently of us, under its own privacy policy. We never receive or store that information ourselves.

Data retention and deletion

On your Mac, PitchKiwi stores a small local record in Keychain: a hash of your license key and the timestamp of your last successful check. Choosing "Deactivate this Mac" in Settings clears it. We hold no copy of this anywhere else, because we have nowhere to hold it.

Children's privacy

PitchKiwi is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the "Last updated" date above will change. Since we have no email on file for you, check back here for updates.

Cookies

This website does not set any first-party tracking or analytics cookies.

Two things load from third-party domains and are worth knowing about:

  • Google Fonts — this site loads web fonts from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. Loading a font this way exposes your IP address to Google as part of that request. No cookie is set by this alone.
  • Checkout page — when you buy, Gumroad may set its own cookies during checkout, under its own cookie and privacy policy.

The PitchKiwi macOS app itself doesn't use cookies at all — it's a native app, not a browser.

Accessibility Statement

We want this site and the PitchKiwi app to be usable by as many people as possible, including people using screen readers, keyboard navigation, or who need higher-contrast text.

  • This site targets WCAG 2.1 AA color contrast for body text and interactive elements.
  • All interactive elements are keyboard-reachable and show a visible focus outline.
  • Images and icons that carry meaning include text alternatives.

If you hit a page or a part of the app that's hard to use with assistive technology, tell us specifically what happened and where — that's the fastest way for a one-person project to actually fix it. Reach out via @AhmedYsrrr.
