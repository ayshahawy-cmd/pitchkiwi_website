See it in action

The actual app: turn rambling into a structured prompt, see what's tracked on-device, and shape text locally without a second model.

Most dictation apps send your voice somewhere else first.

Typing a detailed prompt is slow, so most people write short ones and get worse results back. Talking is faster, but the popular dictation apps process your audio on their servers before it ever reaches your screen. That's a strange trade for developers: you end up reading file paths, internal plans, and half-written code out loud to a company you've never met.

01

Cloud transcription means your audio leaves your Mac before you see any text.

02

Generic dictation cleans up prose, not the syntax, structure, and terminology developers use.

03

Generic dictation mangles the exact things developers say all day: useEffect, --force, file paths, casing.

PitchKiwi does the transcription itself, on your Mac.

Hold a hotkey, speak, release. The speech model runs locally. A deterministic cleanup pass fixes punctuation, casing, and developer terms built for how developers actually talk. Nothing is uploaded, because there's nowhere for it to go, which means your code, prompts, and internal plans stay exactly as secure as your own Mac.

A prompt, not just a transcript.

Ramble for a minute about what you want built, half-formed thoughts and all. PitchKiwi turns the mess into Goal, Context, Requirements, and Done when, ready to paste into Claude Code or any agent.

  • Only what you actually said. It never invents requirements you didn't give it.
  • Runs entirely on your Mac. The raw ramble never leaves it, let alone reaches a second model.
  • Paste-ready. No cleanup pass of your own before your agent can use it.
prompt mode
"okay so I want the settings page to have like a dark mode toggle at the top and it should remember what you picked even after you close the app also make sure it matches the rest of the sidebar styling"
Goal: add a dark mode toggle to the settings page
Requirements:
- Toggle placed at the top of the settings page
- Preference persists across app restarts
- Matches existing sidebar styling
Done when: toggling works and the choice survives a restart
